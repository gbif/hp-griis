<p>
The Global Register of Introduced and Invasive Species (GRIIS) presents verified checklists of introduced (alien) and invasive alien species. Included are national checklists (countries that are Party to the Convention on Biological Diversity (CBD), as well as non-party countries), and sub-national checklists such as overseas territories, checklists of protected and other designated areas such as Marine bioregions, as well as islands.
</p>
<p>
GRIIS checklists are based on a published methodology and are supported by the Global Biodiversity Information Facility (GBIF) Integrated Publishing Tool, that jointly enable ongoing improvements and updates to expand their taxonomic coverage and completeness. Taxonomic coverage includes all kingdoms of organisms occurring in all environments and systems.
</p>
<p>
Checklists are reviewed by networks of country or species experts. Verified checklists, as well as those under review, are published through the GBIF Integrated Publishing Tool, and presented for export on the GRIIS website in Excel format. GRIIS Checklists available for export include the verbaitm scientific name from the source information and the taxonomic rank and status. Synonyms are identified and the Accepted name listed. This detail is not available on the GBIF published checklists. These checklists include a list of Accepted names of species identified as introduced and invasive in that location.
</p>
<p>
GRIIS checklists are presented in ChecklistBank a joint development between Catalogue of Life (COL) and GBIF, ChecklistBank supports the publication and curation of checklists and provides a platform for their consistent discovery, use and citation
</p>
